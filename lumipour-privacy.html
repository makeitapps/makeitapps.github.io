1. Who we are

LumiPour – Color Sort Puzzle ("the game") is published by MakeITapps, an independent app and game developer based in Finland ("we", "us"). MakeITapps is the data controller for the game. If you have any questions about this policy or your data, contact us at marko.saaristo@hotmail.fi.

2. Summary

  • The game does not require an account, login or registration.
  • We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.
  • Your game progress and settings are stored only on your own device. We do not run any servers that receive them.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may process limited device data to deliver ads, as described below.
  • Because the game is made for players of all ages, ads are requested as child-directed: no personalized ads, and the game does not use the device's advertising ID.

3. Data stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the game saves the following data locally on your device: unlocked levels, stars and best move counts, the level you are currently playing (bottle contents, move count and undo history), and your settings (music and sound volume, vibration, language, reduced motion, color symbols and similar options).

This data never leaves your device through the game and is not sent to us. You can delete it at any time with Settings → Reset progress, by clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the game.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and is supported by ads provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited). The game shows a banner while playing, occasional full-screen ads between levels, and an optional video ad that you can choose to watch to receive an extra bottle.

When an ad is requested or shown, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and process information such as:

  • IP address (which may be used to estimate an approximate, general location such as country),
  • device and app information (for example device model, operating system version, language, app version),
  • ad interactions (for example ad views and taps) and diagnostic data (for example performance and crash information related to ads).

Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads, limit how often you see the same ad, and prevent fraud and abuse. We do not receive this information in a form that identifies you; we only see aggregated reports such as the number of ad impressions.

Settings for a general audience including children. All ad requests from the game are tagged for child-directed treatment and limited to ads suitable for all audiences (G rating). With this setting, Google does not serve personalized (interest-based) ads or remarketing, and the game does not access the Android advertising ID.

More information: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

5. Consent (EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent message (User Messaging Platform) before any ads are requested, where required by law. You can review or change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy settings inside the game. The legal basis for processing is your consent and, for the operation of the game itself, our legitimate interest in providing a free, ad-supported game.

6. Permissions

The game itself needs no special permissions. The advertising SDK uses network access (INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) to load ads. The game works fully offline; ads are simply not shown without an internet connection. The game does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, files, or location.

7. Children

LumiPour is suitable for players of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game has no chat, no user accounts, no social features and no in-game purchases, and ads are configured as described in section 4. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that personal information about your child has been collected, please contact us and we will help to have it deleted.

8. Data sharing and transfers

We do not sell personal data and we do not share data with anyone other than Google for the advertising described above. Google may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States, under its own safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

9. Data retention

Data stored on your device is kept until you reset your progress, clear the app's data or uninstall the game. Data processed by Google for advertising is retained according to Google's own policies.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU General Data Protection Regulation), you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, to data portability, and to withdraw your consent at any time. Because we do not hold personal data about you on our own systems, most requests can be handled directly on your device as described above; for ad-related data you can also use Google's tools. You may contact us with any request, and you have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. In Finland this is the Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuoja.fi).

11. Security

Game data is stored in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. Communication between the advertising SDK and Google is encrypted in transit.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if the game gets new features. The current version is always available on this page and the effective date at the top shows when it was last changed.
